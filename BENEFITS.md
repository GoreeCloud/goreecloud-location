# GoreeCloud Location — Benefits

GoreeCloud Location is designed to provide a first-party location platform whose privacy, identity, recovery, and continuity behavior is governed by GoreeCloud rather than by advertising or third-party account ecosystems.

Primary intended benefits:

- Private-by-default ownership of precise location records.
- One native GoreeCloud boundary for live tracking, history, device finding, and future sharing/recovery workflows.
- Server-enforced device revocation and tracking pause rather than client-only controls.
- Replaceable mapping/geocoding/routing dependencies to reduce vendor lock-in.
- Open-format portability and Everkeep-aligned continuity objectives.
- GoreeCloud Identity integration for consistent account/device authority.
- Privacy Shield and Wardveil governance for highly sensitive location operations.
- Glaze UI 2.0+ accessibility and interaction consistency when rendered acceptance is complete.

These are architectural/product objectives unless the corresponding capability is explicitly marked implemented and validated in source or deployment evidence.
