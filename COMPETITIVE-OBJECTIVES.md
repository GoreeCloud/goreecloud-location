# GoreeCloud Location — Competitive Objectives

Capability references include Google Maps/Timeline, Apple Find My, and self-hosted location tools, but GoreeCloud Location must remain an original GoreeCloud-owned implementation.

Objectives:

- Match strong consumer location-history and device-finding usability without monetizing precise location data.
- Provide first-party live/history ownership, explicit sharing, and recovery controls with stronger privacy visibility.
- Make self-hosting, portability, backup, and recovery normal product capabilities rather than afterthoughts.
- Keep mapping, geocoding, and routing providers replaceable.
- Integrate account/device authority through GoreeCloud Identity and cross-platform governance through Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and Glaze UI.
- Treat anti-stalking and abuse resistance as mandatory technical acceptance criteria for Find My expansion.
- Support accessible, responsive Glaze UI 2.0+ experiences across approved GoreeCloud clients.

Competitive parity is not a claim of present implementation. Stable claims require implementation and acceptance evidence.
